About Aurelia

Fifteen years of exceptional living

We are a boutique advisory for luxury residential and investment real estate — built on discretion, market intelligence and enduring client relationships.

Our Story

A different kind of real estate agency.

Aurelia Estates was founded in Mumbai in 2011 with a simple conviction: that buying or selling an exceptional home deserves the same rigour and discretion as any significant investment.

Today our advisors cover more than 35 prime locations across six cities — from the sea-facing apartments of Bandra and Worli to the villas of Jubilee Hills and the beach homes of North Goa. We remain deliberately boutique, representing a limited number of clients at a time so that every mandate receives senior attention.
